DigiBookMaker
=============

1. Double-click DigiBookMaker.exe (keep it inside this folder; it needs the _internal folder next to it).
2. Click "Add photos..." (or drag your page photos onto the list). Check the order.
3. Press "Make book".
4. Press "Open PDF".

Your books are saved in Documents\DigiBookMaker.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
(The app is not signed with a paid certificate; that is the only reason for the warning.)
